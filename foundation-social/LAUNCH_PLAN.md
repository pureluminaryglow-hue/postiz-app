## Ten launch-ready draft concepts
These are drafts for adaptation in Postiz. Any reference to Brandon's personal interests requires the family's approved story and applicable consent; do not publish sensitive support details.

### 1. Foundation introduction (all core channels)
**Hook:** What happens to autistic adults when the people who care for them are no longer here?
**Caption:** It's a question too many South African families face. The Marion Hughes Foundation is being developed to help create safer, more dignified futures for autistic adults. Our first planned project is Brandon's Home: a supported-living model built around safety, choice, meaningful daily life and lasting support. This is the beginning of a long journey. Follow along and help start the conversation.
**CTA:** Share this with someone who believes every adult deserves belonging.

### 2. Brandon's story (Facebook, Instagram, TikTok)
**Hook:** Brandon taught us that purpose can start with something as ordinary as washing clothes.
**Caption:** Brandon loves laundry and washing machines. That joy sparked a bigger question: what if supported living was designed around people's own interests and strengths, instead of expecting everyone to fit the same routine? Brandon's Home is our answer in the making.
**CTA:** Help us bring this vision to more families.

### 3. Beyond the school years (carousel)
**Slides:** (1) What happens after school? (2) Adult support is a lifelong need. (3) Families need trusted options. (4) Autistic adults need safety, agency and purpose. (5) Help build the conversation.
**CTA:** Save and share.

### 4. The supported-home vision (LinkedIn / Facebook)
**Caption:** Brandon's Home is a planned supported-living pilot for autistic adults with substantial day-to-day support needs. The model aims to combine an appropriately staffed safe home, individually tailored care, meaningful activities and long-term governance. We are at the planning and fundraising stage, and welcome responsible partners who can help with property, safeguarding, services, training and sustainability.
**CTA:** Partnership enquiries: brandonshome@marionhughesfoundation.co.site

### 5. Inclusion is not one-size-fits-all (Reel)
**Hook:** Dignity doesn't look identical for everyone.
**Caption:** Some adults thrive with independent work. Others need hands-on support to live safely and take part in activities they enjoy. Both deserve choices, respect and opportunities for meaningful days.
**CTA:** Share what inclusion means to you.

### 6. Our first milestone (all channels)
**Caption:** Our immediate focus is planning the first Brandon's Home pilot, building trustworthy governance and raising initial project funds. Every milestone will be shared transparently. We're not asking anyone to believe in a finished project; we're inviting supporters to help build it responsibly.
**CTA:** Visit our verified BackaBuddy campaign to support the first steps.

### 7. Corporate partnership invitation (LinkedIn)
**Hook:** A sponsorship can help build more than a building.
**Caption:** We are seeking corporate partners to explore practical, sustainable support for an autism-focused supported-living pilot in South Africa. Opportunities to discuss include property, equipment, skills development, accessible work activities, professional services and long-term operating sustainability.
**CTA:** Contact brandonshome@marionhughesfoundation.co.site

### 8. Support beyond parents (YouTube / Facebook)
**Hook:** The question that keeps families awake: who will be there when we can't be?
**Caption:** Long-term planning for adults with substantial support needs should not fall on families alone. Through Brandon's Home, we hope to help create a model that protects dignity, safety and everyday quality of life for the years ahead.
**CTA:** Share and support the conversation.

### 9. No contribution too small (Instagram / Facebook)
**Caption:** Not everyone can donate. You can still help. Share our story, introduce us to a potential partner or tell someone why supported living for autistic adults matters. Awareness is not the finish line — it is how we find the people who can help turn a plan into action.
**CTA:** Share or support via our verified campaign link.
